Kasama

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Kasama is a calm personal assistant app. This page explains, in plain words, what Kasama keeps, what it sends, and how you can delete it. The short version: your lists live on your phone. What you tell Kasama is sent to its AI only to get an answer. There are no ads, and nothing is sold.

What stays on your phone

These are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your tasks, ideas, notes and chat history
  • Your settings: name, schedule, reminder times, place, voice language, faith and support choices
  • "What I know about you" (Kasama's memory, which you can read and edit in the app)
  • Your moods from the evening check-in
  • "Your people": the names and contact handles you add
  • Reminders and alarms (they are scheduled by your phone itself)

What is sent when you talk to Kasama

When you send a message, sort your thoughts or plan your day, the app sends only what the AI needs to answer:

  • Your message, and a few recent messages from the same chat
  • Up to 30 of your open tasks (titles, due dates, priority)
  • Your fixed schedule, your name, and a short part of "What I know about you"
  • The first names of "your people" (never their phone numbers or handles)
  • If you connect your calendar: only the times you are busy today, labeled "Busy". Event names, places and guests never leave your phone.
  • The current date, time and time zone

This goes to two services that run Kasama:

  • Supabase hosts Kasama's backend. It gives each phone an anonymous account (no email, no password), counts how many messages each phone sends per day so the service stays free of abuse, and stores replies you choose to report. It does not store your messages.
  • Anthropic provides Claude, the AI that writes Kasama's replies. Your request is processed to create the answer. Anthropic's handling of this data is described in Anthropic's privacy policy; under its commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default.

Voice

When you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech service (for example Google's, or your phone maker's) turns your voice into text. Kasama receives only the text. Kasama does not record or keep audio. Your phone's speech service may process the audio under its own privacy policy.

Microphone, calendar and notification permissions

  • Microphone: only while you are using voice input, after you tap the mic.
  • Calendar: only if you connect it, to plan around your busy times. Kasama reads when you're busy and does not change your calendar.
  • Notifications and alarms: for your reminders, morning hello, evening check-in and alarms you ask for.

Reports

If you tap "Report" on a reply, only that reply and the reason you chose are saved, so it can be checked and Kasama can be improved.

What Kasama never does

  • No ads, no tracking for advertising, no selling or sharing your data with advertisers
  • No contacts list access: Kasama only knows the people you type in yourself
  • It never sends messages for you. "Say hi" opens your own messaging app.

Safety

Kasama is not a doctor, therapist or emergency service. If you are in danger or thinking of hurting yourself, please contact the NCMH Crisis Hotline (1553, or 0917-899-8727 / 0919-057-1553, free, 24/7), or call 911.

Delete your data

In the app: You → Your data → Delete my data. This erases everything on your phone and your anonymous account on the backend, including its usage counts. Reports you sent are kept only without any link to you. For other ways, see Delete your Kasama data.

Children

Kasama is meant for people 18 and older. It is not designed for children.
