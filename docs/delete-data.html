Delete your Kasama data

For the Kasama app by Jaypee Vilador

The fastest way: in the app

  1. Open Kasama and go to the You tab.
  2. Scroll down to Your data and tap Delete my data.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

This erases right away:

  • On your phone: tasks, ideas, notes, chat history, memory, moods, settings, your people, reminders and alarms
  • On the backend: your phone's anonymous account and its daily usage counts

Replies you reported are kept only without any link to you, so they can still be reviewed.

If you no longer have the app

Uninstalling Kasama erases everything stored on your phone. Your anonymous account on the backend has no name, email or phone number. To have it removed as well, email jaypee.vilador@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Kasama data". Requests are handled within 30 days.
